2022: The Push Power Era

Every elite venture roots from raw execution. Alexander Yard Works launched in 2022 utilizing a basic manual push mower. Moving door-to-door, we learned fine mechanical diagnostics, mastered clean cutting matrices, and systematically built a loyal neighborhood footprint.

The Ride-On Scale Up

As contract volume surged, we scaled up infrastructure. By incorporating commercial-grade ride-on machinery into our fleet, operational velocity tripled. This allowed us to manage extensive rural lots, optimize weekly routing schedules, and maintain top-tier consistency.
