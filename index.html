The Origin Engine

Every great project starts with minimal overhead. In 2022, Alexander Yard Works kicked off with nothing more than a single, basic push mower and a solid work ethic. Footing it door-to-door, we learned the subtle nuances of precise cutting patterns and built our core community clientele from the ground up.

Scaling the Fleet

As yard demands grew, manual pushing wasn't fast enough. We reinvested into high-efficiency ride-on mowers. This technological leap scaled up our output speed exponentially, allowing us to take on massive commercial-sized rural properties and drastically optimize our scheduling footprint.
